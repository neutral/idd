# Evidence Capture

Capture this claim type in the shared Arc evidence shape: claim, context or
method, observations and raw artifacts, and verdict.

## Claim

- state the authoritative source, owner, or decision
- name the question it governed
- record the affected refs or downstream work

## Methodology References

- record the claim type folder path
- link `arc.md`, relevant step refs, and any decision refs
- link run-ledger entries where the authority basis mattered

## Method Or Context

- explain why the authority choice became necessary
- record whether it was inherited from the Arc plan or chosen during a step

## Observations

- record the competing sources or nearby alternatives
- record why the chosen authority basis controlled the current run
- record any limit on the authority claim

## Raw Artifacts

- decision artifacts
- user direction or owner approval notes
- intent, blueprint, or code refs that were in tension

## Verdict Guidance

- `supported`: the authority basis is explicit and inspectable
- `mixed`: a usable basis exists but some ambiguity remains
- `open`: the basis is still waiting on clarification or approval
- `incomplete`: the authority thread exists but lacks enough support
- `failed`: the Arc treated authority as settled without inspectable support

## Evidence Thread Rules

- create a new item when a separate authority question becomes independent
- enrich the existing item when the same authority choice gains more support
- do not mix authority basis with the full conflict narrative when a real
  conflict needs its own thread

## Closeout Conditions

- the chosen authority basis is explicit
- the controlled question and affected refs are preserved
- any durable authority outcome has a decision-home when needed
