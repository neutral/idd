# Claim Type

Claim type name: `governance-ownership`
Summary: preserve what approval, explicit owner, or consequential follow-up
posture applies to the Arc.
Why this claim matters: later trust depends on whether consequential choices had
the right approval or an explicit owner.
Primary trust question: can a later reader inspect the approval or ownership
posture for consequential Arc outcomes?
Default evidence kind: governance-and-ownership proof thread

## Use This Claim When

- the Arc makes a consequential decision or tradeoff
- approval is required before closeout
- follow-up ownership materially affects trust in the outcome

## Claim Sentence Template

- `<Arc or scoped work>` was approved by `<approval evidence or owner>`, and
  follow-up ownership rests with `<owner or team>`.

## Required Inputs

- `arc.md`
- relevant step files
- approval notes, owner decisions, or follow-up records

## Update These Artifacts When

- the Arc evidence index needs current governance coverage
- a step summary should cite the governance evidence it relied on
- a durable approval or owner path must be copied into a decision artifact
