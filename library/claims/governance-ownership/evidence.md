# Evidence Capture

Capture this claim type in the shared Arc evidence shape: claim, context or
method, observations and raw artifacts, and verdict.

## Claim

- state the approval, owner assignment, or both
- record the consequential area or follow-up it governs
- record any limit on the governance claim

## Methodology References

- record the claim type folder path
- link `arc.md`, relevant step refs, and the evidence index
- link approval notes, owner assignments, or decision refs

## Method Or Context

- explain why approval or ownership mattered in this Arc
- record whether the claim is about approval, ownership, or both

## Observations

- record the approval evidence or explicit owner posture
- distinguish observed evidence from expected but missing evidence
- record any deferred governance point

## Raw Artifacts

- approval comments or notes
- owner assignments
- decision artifacts or issue refs

## Verdict Guidance

- `supported`: the approval or ownership posture is explicit and inspectable
- `mixed`: some governance support exists but a material gap remains
- `open`: governance is waiting on pending input
- `incomplete`: the thread exists but lacks enough support yet
- `failed`: the Arc presents consequential work without inspectable governance

## Evidence Thread Rules

- create a new item when approval and ownership become independent questions
- enrich the existing item when the same governance thread gains more support
- do not mix governance evidence with general authority or verification posture

## Closeout Conditions

- the governance posture is explicit
- the supporting approval or owner path is inspectable
- any deferred governance obligation is preserved
