# Claim Type

Claim type name: `residual-uncertainty`
Summary: preserve what important assumptions, skipped checks, open questions,
or deferred work still limit trust in the Arc.
Why this claim matters: later readers should not have to infer uncertainty from
scattered notes.
Primary trust question: what meaningful uncertainty still remains, and how
should it limit trust or next actions?
Default evidence kind: residual-risk proof thread

## Use This Claim When

- important assumptions remain unverified
- checks were skipped or blocked
- the Arc carries forward deferred work that still limits trust

## Claim Sentence Template

- `<Arc or scoped work>` remains limited by `<assumption, skipped check, open
  question, or deferred work>`, which affects `<scope of trust or next action>`.

## Required Inputs

- `arc.md`
- relevant step files
- verification notes, deferred-work notes, or open questions

## Update These Artifacts When

- the Arc evidence index needs current uncertainty coverage
- a step summary should cite the uncertainty evidence it relied on
- final validation needs explicit carry-forward limits
