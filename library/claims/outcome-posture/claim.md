# Claim Type

Claim type name: `outcome-posture`
Summary: preserve why the Arc outcome is blocked, partial, mixed, or only
conditionally acceptable.
Why this claim matters: later readers need an explicit overall verdict when the
Arc is not cleanly supported.
Primary trust question: what is the current overall Arc posture, and why is it
not simply supported?
Default evidence kind: overall-outcome proof thread

## Use This Claim When

- the Arc is blocked, partial, mixed, or conditionally acceptable
- final validation needs an explicit overall posture
- the result is not cleanly represented by other claim threads alone

## Claim Sentence Template

- `<Arc or scoped work>` is currently `<blocked, partial, mixed, or
  conditionally acceptable>` because `<main limiting factors>`.

## Required Inputs

- `arc.md`
- relevant step files
- supporting evidence threads that drive the posture

## Update These Artifacts When

- the Arc evidence index needs the current overall posture
- final validation or closeout needs an explicit overall verdict
- carry-forward obligations depend on the current posture
