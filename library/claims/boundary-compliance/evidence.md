# Evidence Capture

Capture this claim type in the shared Arc evidence shape: claim, context or
method, observations and raw artifacts, and verdict.

## Claim

- state the effective operating scope and permission posture
- name the touched or deliberately untouched paths
- record how out-of-scope discoveries were handled

## Methodology References

- record the claim type folder path
- link `.methodologies/idd/status.md`, `arc.md`, and relevant step refs
- link run-ledger entries that stopped, deferred, or narrowed work

## Method Or Context

- explain how effective scope and permissions were determined
- note whether a boundary issue changed the Arc plan

## Observations

- record the in-scope roots and permission posture that mattered
- record related surfaces intentionally left untouched
- record any stop or escalation triggered by the boundary

## Raw Artifacts

- relevant `status.md` paths
- step summaries and ledger entries
- decisions or notes that approved a boundary-sensitive move

## Verdict Guidance

- `supported`: the Arc stayed within the declared boundary
- `mixed`: most work stayed in bounds but a material exception or ambiguity
  remains
- `open`: the final boundary posture depends on pending clarification
- `incomplete`: the boundary thread exists but is not yet inspectable enough
- `failed`: the Arc crossed a disallowed boundary or hid the conflict

## Evidence Thread Rules

- create a new item when an independent boundary issue needs separate handling
- enrich the existing item when the same boundary posture gains more support
- do not mix boundary posture with authority choice or final outcome posture

## Closeout Conditions

- the effective boundary is explicit
- the touched and untouched surfaces are inspectable
- any out-of-scope discovery is preserved with its handling
